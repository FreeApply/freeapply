        Job sites
        Choose where FreeApply may search and apply for you. For sites with an account, open the site and sign in yourself in the browser your AI assistant uses, then tick "I'm signed in". Save the password in that browser when it offers, and it fills it in if a site asks you to sign in again. FreeApply never sees or stores job-site passwords. If a site signs you out, FreeApply keeps going on your other sites and asks you to sign in again.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        

        
        
      
        
      
        Your email
        FreeApply reads your email for employer replies (interview invites, rejections, offers) and tracks them for you. Connect Gmail with an app password: it stays on this computer and your AI assistant never sees it. If you'd rather not, choose "I'll enter all passwords myself": you then sign in to every site yourself, and replies can come through your AI app's Gmail connector.
        
        
        
      
        
      
        You're set
        FreeApply now keeps applying on its own from your AI app: it begins within seconds, so leave that conversation open (if it doesn't begin, tell your AI assistant "set up FreeApply"). Start and Stop are at the top of every page, you can change anything later in Settings, and the Needs you tab lists anything FreeApply needs from you.
