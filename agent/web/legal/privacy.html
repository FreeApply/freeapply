This policy explains what FreeApply does with information about you. FreeApply is published by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us"). In short: your job-search data stays on your computer. We receive only the categories described in section 2, and you control each one in Settings → Data sharing. Your contact details and resume leave your computer only if you join the optional Recruiter Talent Pool. We never receive your eligibility answers, demographic information, email content or passwords.

1. Data that stays on your computer

FreeApply stores the following only in its data folder on your device (by default ~/.freeapply). We cannot access it:

  • Profile: name, email, phone, city, region, country, LinkedIn and portfolio links.
  • Job targets: desired roles, fields, seniority, pay floor, locations, work modes and keywords.
  • Eligibility: work authorization, sponsorship need, security-clearance status, start date and years of experience.
  • Your resume files and the resume text your AI assistant extracts.
  • Job-tracking records: jobs found and applied to, application status, confirmation text, notes and documents used.
  • Email reply records: sender, subject, message identifiers, date, type of reply and a short summary.
  • A problem log: what failed in FreeApply and its error messages, with personal data removed (see "Problem reports" in section 2). It keeps the 30 most recent problems, until you send or dismiss each one.

You can view, edit and export this data in the dashboard, and delete all of it by deleting the data folder.

Sponsored cards. FreeApply is free and shows a sponsored card in the dashboard, clearly labeled. A few times a day the app checks our server for the current list of sponsored cards (an unchanged list is not downloaded again) and picks one on your computer, using your job targets there. No information about you is sent to choose or show a card, and no advertising scripts or trackers run in the dashboard. Like any download, the request shows your IP address to our network provider and our server, which use it only to deliver the file and prevent abuse. Only when you click a card does your browser visit the sponsor's site, which then handles your visit under its own policy. If you allow anonymous usage statistics, they include how many times each card was shown and clicked on your computer.

Updates. About once a day the app also checks our server for a newer version of its signed instructions and downloads them if there is one. The request carries only the app's version number; like the sponsored-card download, it shows your IP address to our network provider and our server, which use it only to deliver the file and prevent abuse.

2. Data we collect

Nothing in this section is collected while its choice is off. Each choice is in Settings → Data sharing, where you can turn it on or off at any time with one click; turning a choice off takes no more steps than turning it on. Where you are decides how each choice starts:

  • United States: anonymous usage statistics and job-market data are collected by default, as stated next to the agreement when you accept these terms. There is no separate prompt at setup; turn either off at any time in Settings → Data sharing. The Talent Pool and product update emails are off unless you turn them on.
  • Everywhere else (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Canada), and whenever your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal: nothing is collected unless you turn it on in Settings → Data sharing.

Your Talent Pool profile and your update-email address are stored under a separate random ID that is never linked to your installation ID, so usage statistics and job-market data stay anonymous.

FreeApply decides where you are from the country in your profile or, if that is empty, from your computer's region setting. It treats you as outside the United States whenever it cannot tell.

Problem reports. When something in FreeApply fails, the app notes it in its problem log after removing your name, email, phone number, resume text, profile links, your home-folder path and the query part of web addresses. Each report is shown in Settings → Data sharing → Problems exactly as it would be sent. A report is sent only if you tick "I agree to share this report with FreeApply" on that report and press Send; nothing is ever sent automatically, a report is never sent without its own consent, and Dismiss deletes it from your computer. A report contains your random installation ID, the FreeApply and workflow versions, what failed (for example the name of the action or the part of the dashboard), the error message, where in FreeApply's code it happened, how many times it happened and when you agreed to send it. We use reports only to find and fix problems in FreeApply. This is separate from the choices in the table: problem reports can be sent with every choice off.

Your resume and contact details are collected only if you join the Talent Pool. Before joining, remove from your resume anything you do not want recruiters to see.

When data is sent, your IP address is seen as part of the connection by our network provider, Cloudflare, and by our server. We do not store IP addresses with the data you share. Our server uses them only briefly, in memory and in short-lived security logs, to prevent abuse.

Our website. Visits to www.mybotsai.com are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies: for each page view, the page, the referring site, the visitor's country, device type, browser and operating system, and how fast the page loaded. We see only totals. This applies to the website only; the FreeApply app sends none of it. Details, including how to avoid being counted, are on the website's Cookies, analytics and ads page.

3. How we use and share shared data

  • We use shared data only for the purposes listed above.
  • Outside the Talent Pool, we do not sell your personal information. We never use it for targeted advertising, and we never use any shared data to make decisions about you.
  • Talent Pool profiles are shown only to recruiters and employers who have accepted our Recruiter Terms. Those terms limit use to recruiting you for jobs, forbid resale and discrimination, and require them to delete your profile when you ask. We do not rate, score or rank you for recruiters. Recruiters see the profile you provided. Once a recruiter has viewed or saved your profile, their own copy is governed by their privacy policy, and leaving the pool stops new access but cannot recall copies already made.
  • We may publish or share job-market statistics only in aggregated or de-identified form that does not identify you, and we commit not to try to re-identify it.
  • Our hosting provider, Contabo GmbH, runs the server that stores shared data and processes it for us as a service provider under its own data-protection terms. Backblaze, Inc. stores encrypted backup copies of that server's database for us (Backblaze B2 cloud storage, in the United States). The copies are encrypted on our server before they are uploaded, with a key that only we hold, so Backblaze cannot read them. Cloudflare, Inc. is our network and security provider: every connection to our website and server, including the app's downloads and the data you choose to share, passes through Cloudflare, which protects the server from attacks, caches public files and processes this traffic for us as a service provider under its own data-protection terms. Cloudflare also counts visits to our website for us (Cloudflare Web Analytics, see section 2).
  • We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a transfer of FreeApply to a successor who agrees to this policy.

4. Your AI assistant, email and other services

FreeApply works with an AI assistant and connectors that you install and authorize, for example Claude and its Gmail connector. Information those services process, including the content of job postings, your resume text and your emails, is handled by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies, not by us. If you connect your email in Settings, FreeApply itself signs in to your mail server (for Gmail, over standard IMAP) with the app password you entered, which is stored only in a file on your computer, to find replies from employers you applied to; the text of matching emails is passed to your AI assistant to sort, as a connector would do. Neither the password nor your emails are sent to us. FreeApply does not use Google APIs and does not send any Google user data to our servers. Employer websites, job boards and applicant-tracking systems you apply through receive the information you submit to them under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention

Usage statistics and job-market records are kept for up to 24 months after the last time your installation shares data, then deleted or fully aggregated. Subscription emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Talent Pool profiles are kept while you are in the pool and deleted from our server within 30 days after you leave, or 12 months after your installation last refreshed the profile, whichever comes first. Problem reports are deleted from our server 90 days after we receive them, or sooner when you delete your shared data. Our server's database is backed up every 6 hours into encrypted copies that expire on their own, so data deleted from our server can remain in those backups for up to 3 weeks. Local data stays on your computer until you delete it.

7. Security

Local data is stored only on your device. The dashboard accepts connections only from your own computer. Data you choose to share is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access restricted to us. No system is perfectly secure; protect your device and accounts. If a breach affects personal information we hold, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.

8. International users

We are based in the United States. Shared data is stored on our server hosted by Contabo, which may process it in the United States or other countries, and encrypted backup copies are stored by Backblaze in the United States. Connections pass through Cloudflare's network, which may handle them in the country nearest to you. By sharing data, you understand that it will be transferred to and processed there.

9. Children

FreeApply is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

This policy carries the version number of the FreeApply release it ships with. If we change it, we will update the effective date, and the dashboard will ask you to accept it again. If a change expands what we collect, we will ask for your consent before collecting it.
