# FreeApply 1.0.0

**Released 2026-10-27.** App, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Disclosures and Recruiter Terms: version 1.0.0.

The first public release. FreeApply finds jobs that fit your settings, applies through the AI app you already use,
tracks every application in a dashboard on your computer and checks Gmail for employer replies.

## What's in FreeApply
- **Finds jobs that fit.** Searches the job sites you choose for roles that match your targets, pay floor and
  locations, and skips jobs and reposts it has already tracked.
- **Applies for you** on employer sites and job boards with your real details, your résumé and answers you saved once.
  Submissions are paced, and a job site that signs you out or shows its own daily limit is paused while the others
  continue.
- **Never invents experience.** Cover letters and answers are checked against your résumé and settings. Anything it
  can't back up is refused, and it asks you instead.
- **Knows when to stop.** CAPTCHAs, new accounts, passwords and questions your settings don't answer go to the Issues
  tab for you. It never types a password, creates an account or solves a CAPTCHA.
- **Dashboard** at `http://127.0.0.1:7878`, reachable only from your computer: Overview, Applications, Replies, Issues
  and Settings, with charts, search and CSV export.
- **Reply checks.** With your AI app's Gmail connector it checks for employer replies every 4 hours (you can change
  this), records acknowledgements, assessments, interviews, rejections and offers, and stars or labels them as you
  choose.
- **Unattended runs** within the time and application limits you set.
- **Signed workflow.** The agent's procedures and limits ship signed with our release key. Any edit locks the tools
  until the official copy is restored, and an update is installed only when its signature checks out.
- **Data sharing is your choice**, in Settings → Data sharing, where you can preview exactly what would be sent and
  delete everything already shared. Your résumé and contact details leave your computer only if you join the
  Recruiter Talent Pool.
- **One sponsored card** in the dashboard, labeled "Sponsored" and chosen on your computer. No ad scripts, no trackers,
  and no data sent to show it.
- Works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf.

## New since the 0.3 previews
- The app and its legal documents now share one version number, starting with 1.0.0. The Terms were 1.0 and the
  Privacy Policy 1.8.
- Accepting the Terms carries over to later versions. The dashboard asks you again only when the text of a legal
  document changes, not with every update.

## Legal
- Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Disclosures and Recruiter Terms: version 1.0.0, effective October 27, 2026.
- Changed text: Terms section 13 (General) and Privacy Policy section 10 (Changes) now say that the version number is
  the FreeApply release the document ships with, and that the dashboard asks you to accept again when the text
  changes. Nothing else changed.

## Upgrading from a preview (0.3.x)
- **Claude Desktop:** download `freeapply.mcpb` from https://www.mybotsai.com/install/ and install it the same way as
  before.
- **Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf:** run the install command from https://www.mybotsai.com/install/ again.
- Your data in `~/.freeapply` stays as it is. The dashboard asks you once to accept the 1.0.0 Terms and Privacy
  Policy, and the agent tools wait until you do. Your data-sharing choices are kept.

## Requirements
- Claude Desktop: nothing else. It runs FreeApply on its own built-in Node.js.
- Other apps: Node.js 22.13 or newer.
- Windows, macOS or Linux.
