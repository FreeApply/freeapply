# FreeApply 1.0.1

**Released 2026-10-27.** App, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Disclosures and Recruiter Terms: version 1.0.1.

Clearer tools for your AI app (22 instead of 25), a Needs you tab that asks each question once in plain words, and problem reports you can send, each only with your OK. Remove jobs from the dashboard. The Privacy Policy changed, so you'll be asked to accept it again.

## New
- **Remove a job** from the dashboard: Applications → Remove deletes it with its notes, form answers and recorded emails,
  after you confirm. Only you can remove jobs; your AI app has no way to.
- **Saved answers from the conversation.** When you answer a form question in chat, your AI can save the answer and reuse it
  on later forms (and remove it again if you ask).
- Needs you: a question several jobs ask now shows up once, and your answer goes to all of them. FreeApply no longer
  stops to ask something you already answered, and a similar question comes with your earlier answer filled in for
  you to check.
- Needs you: jobs whose account is made on the same sign-up page share one card, and one "I made the account" sends
  them all back in the queue. Different employers on a shared careers site keep separate cards.
- Problem reports: when something in FreeApply goes wrong, it is noted under Settings → Data sharing → Problems, with
  your name, contact details, resume text, profile links and home-folder path removed. Each report shows exactly what
  would be sent and goes to FreeApply only if you tick "I agree to share this report with FreeApply" and press Send;
  Dismiss deletes it. Nothing is ever sent on its own.

## Changed
- FreeApply now gives your AI app 22 tools instead of 25: the résumé, Gmail reply-check and data-sharing tools were each
  merged into one. Every tool has a clearer description, a title, an explanation of each setting with its allowed values,
  and marks for what it can change, so your AI picks the right step more often (for example, recording an employer email
  rather than only setting the outcome). Each tool also says exactly what it returns, and long job lists can be read
  page by page.
- The signed instructions your AI follows were updated for the new tools. FreeApply 1.0.0 keeps its own instructions
  until you update.
- FreeApply's source code is now released under the MIT License (`LICENSE` in the repository). The FreeApply name and
  logo are not covered, and the Terms of Service still govern use of FreeApply and of the services we host.
- Needs you: questions are written as short, plain questions you answer by typing, from a dropdown or by picking
  options; the form's own wording and FreeApply's notes are under Advanced. Blocked jobs say plainly what the site
  asked for (a "prove you're a person" check, a code, a warning) and offer "I applied myself", "Try again later" and
  "Skip this job". The account email is picked on the card instead of in a pop-up.
- Dashboard: the Terms, Privacy Policy, Disclosures and Recruiter Terms now open from a footer at the bottom of every
  page, in both Simple and Advanced view, instead of from tabs in the top menu.

## Legal
- Privacy Policy: a new "Problem reports" part says what a report contains, that each one is sent only with its own
  consent, and that sent reports are deleted from our server after 90 days. Because the text changed, you are asked to
  accept the documents again.

## Upgrading
Open the dashboard once and accept the updated Privacy Policy; FreeApply's tools wait until you do.
